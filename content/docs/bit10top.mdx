This index fund tracks the top 10 crypto tokens, providing investors with a broad and diversified exposure to the leading assets in the cryptocurrency market. By investing in BIT10.TOP, participants can benefit from the collective performance of these top tokens, which are selected based on market capitalization, trading volume, and overall influence in the crypto ecosystem.

The top 10 tokens are regularly evaluated to ensure that the fund reflects the most relevant and impactful assets in the market. This strategy aims to minimize risk while maximizing potential returns, making BIT10.TOP an attractive option for both new and experienced investors. BIT10.TOP is currently on Testnet for users to interact with and we show the mechanism for the allocation and the collateral down as well.

We get asked a lot why we chose the simple average for our index fund as oppose to the market cap index fund like the S&P500, this is primarily because we think that the gains from the smaller cryptos will be more than the big ones like Bitcoin and Ethereum who have a significant part of the market and we also have proof of this data coming up.
