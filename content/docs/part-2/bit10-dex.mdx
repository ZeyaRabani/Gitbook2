There are some cross-chain DEX’s that exist but all of them have the same problem where it’s simply a bridge and not a fully decentralized DEX.

BIT10 DEX solves this.

BIT10 DEX is fast, decentralized, and designed to make trading feel like a breeze. Whether you’re new to DeFi or a total pro, this is your ticket to easy, cross-chain action. We offer 5 different layer 1’s to be traded directly with each, this includes Ethereum, Binance Smart chain, Solana, Tron and Cardano.

Using ICP’s infrastructure, there is no need for a bridge as is the case with every other cross-chain DEX, we’ll be using ICP layer-0 infrastructure to connect to each layer 1 natively and complete the transaction that way.

The fees on every other chain can be big, with us it’s only 1 and it’s a small just like we do for our Index funds.
