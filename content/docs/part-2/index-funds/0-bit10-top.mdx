Our flagship fund is BIT10.TOP and makes investing in the top 10 crypto tokens (excluding stablecoins) the simplest it has ever been.

A couple key points that BIT10.TOP does which supercharges your portfolio.
- <b>10 tokens into 1 token = Diversification.</b>

    BIT10.TOP gives you instant exposure to the top 10 crypto tokens by market capitalization, excluding stablecoins. There is no need to stress if 1 token is doing good or not, investing into 10 tokens gives you diversification which spreads the risk and keeps things stable.

    But the TLDR on this is they are both market cap weighted just like the regular S&P500.
    
    Note: In the beginning, BIT10 did equal weighting in its index funds but this has now been changed completely as the data suggests doing it by market cap gives a better indication of how the crypto market is doing but more importantly it allows the users to achieve amazing gains over the long run which beats Bitcoin, S&P500 and Gold.
- <b>10 tokens into 1 token = 1 Fee.</b>

    Buying 10 different tokens individually means 10 different fees that the users have to pay, this is expensive and time consuming, but let’s not forget if the users are dollar cost averaging, that means they are paying even more fees. BIT10.TOP solves this problem by bundling the entire thing into 1 purchase and all into 1 fee and plus if the users are dollar cost averaging there is still only 1 fee they have to pay instead of 10 separate ones. This was always the biggest issue that the founders of BIT10 were facing every day when buying crypto tokens, and BIT10.TOP has solved this problem. 
- <b>Auto-rebalancing</b>

    When you look at the graph of the S&P500 over time(10+ years) it seems to always go up and yes American stocks have been doing really well for the last 100 years but the main reason why the S&P500 works is because of it’s rebalancing mechanism. Every quarter, companies that meet the criteria get added into the S&P500 and other companies get taken out which it’s always the top 500 US public companies in the S&P500, this means that companies that are performing well are coming into the fund and initially taking a small weight in the index fund but as they grow their weight  increases.
    
    A great example is NVIDIA, 10 years ago they were not in the S&P500(Added in 2017) but as of today it has the highest market capitalization and the highest weight in the most traded index fund in the world. Compare that with KODAK which in the 1990’s was one of the biggest companies in the world but they didn't embrace digital technologies and ultimately was removed of the S&P500 in 2004.
    
    BIT10.TOP does something similar except our auto-rebalancing is done every week and we adjust the collateral to meet the weights at the same time as the rebalancing. 
    
    The full details are available on [this page](/part-4/auto-rebalancing) where you can see the all the previous rebalancing to get a better idea.
