For selling BIT10 navigate to [bit10.app/swap?mode=mint](https://https://www.bit10.app/swap?mode=mint).

### How to Reverse Swap

1. Navigate to the BIT10 Swap page [bit10.app/swap](https://https://www.bit10.app/swap) and click on the Reverse Icon to perform a reverse swap.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/mainnet_reverse_swap_1.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

2. Select the number and type of BIT10 tokens you want to send, along with the token you want to receive for the Reverse Swap.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/mainnet_reverse_swap_2.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

3. Click on the Reverse Swap button to sell BIT10 tokens.

4. Approve the connection request for the token and swap canister.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/mainnet_reverse_swap_3.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

5. Then approve the ICRC2 Approve request to approve the token transfer.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/mainnet_reverse_swap_4.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

6. Once the request is approved, the Reverse Swap will begin.

<Callout>NOTE: It may take up to 30 seconds to complete the Reverse Swap. You will receive a notification when the Reverse Swap is successful.</Callout>

7. Once the swap is successful, navigate to the **Portfolio** page to see the result of your transaction under the **Your Recent Activity** section.
