## Crypto has grown up

Crypto has evolved from a niche experiment into a multi-trillion-dollar asset class, but investing in it still feels stuck in 2016. BIT10 is redefining this landscape by bringing the simplicity and efficiency of traditional index funds to crypto, making it easier for anyone to own the market without the hassle of picking winners or timing trades.

## The Evolution of Crypto Investing

The cryptocurrency market has matured significantly over the past decade. What started as a niche technology experiment has become a legitimate asset class recognized by institutions, governments, and millions of retail investors worldwide.

Yet despite this growth, the tools available to everyday investors remain fragmented, complex, and expensive. BIT10 changes that.

## What is BIT10?

BIT10 is a decentralized asset manager specializing in crypto index funds, instead of picking individual tokens and timing the market, with BIT10 TOP you own the crypto market in a single auto-rebalanced token.

| Traditional Crypto Investing | BIT10 Approach |
| --- | --- |
| Pick individual coins | Own the market in one token |
| Time buys and sells | Set and forget |
| Pay 10+ transaction fees | One fee, one transaction |
| Manual rebalancing | Auto-rebalances Daily |
