You can visit Instant Liquidity Providers page [testnet.bit10.app/liquidity-hub/ilp](https://testnet.bit10.app/liquidity-hub/ilp) to become an AP. You can get some tBTC from the [Bitcoin faucet](https://coinfaucet.eu/en/btc-testnet). On ILP you can come and swap tBTC for ckUSDC token. on ILP you can directly provide liquidity for the BIT10 token whihout staking the token and earn incentive. We will directly airdrop you the reward for the ILP swap.

### How to swap on ILP

1. Connect you BTC wallet

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/ilp_1.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

2. Select the token you want to spent and enter the number of token you want to provide liquidity for.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/ilp_2.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

3. Select the type of token you want to recieve.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/ilp_3.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

4. Click on the Swap button to get the token you want to receive.

5. Approve the connection request for the ILP canister.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/ilp_4.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

6. Approve the transaction request for the token you want to provide liquidity for.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/ilp_5.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

7. Once the request is approved, the swap will begin.

<Callout>NOTE: It may take up to 30 min to verifiy the transaction.</Callout>

8. Once the swap is successful, and transaction is verified you will receve the token, and we will airdrop the incentive driectly to you.
