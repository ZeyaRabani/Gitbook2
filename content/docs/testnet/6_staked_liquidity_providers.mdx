You can visit Staked Liquidity Providers page [testnet.bit10.app/liquidity-hub/slp](https://testnet.bit10.app/liquidity-hub/slp) to become an AP. On SLP, you can stake your tokens to earn extra incentives for the staking duration. The more you stake, the more rewards you receive. Rewards can be claimed weekly.

### How to stake on SLP

1. Select the token you want to stake, the amount you wish to stake, and the staking duration.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/slp_1.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

2. Click on the Stake button to stake your Test BIT10 token.

3. Approve the connection request for the token and liquidity hub canister.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/slp_2.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

4. Then approve the ICRC2 Approve request to approve the token transfer.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/slp_3.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

5. Once the request is approved, the staking will begin.

<Callout>NOTE: It may take up to 30 seconds complete the stake. You will receive a notification when the staking is successful.</Callout>

6. Once the stakeing is successful, you can view the Staked Liquidity History.

### How to withdraw on SLP

1. Click on the **Withdraw & Claim Tokens** button.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/slp_wi_1.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

2. Select the token and the amount you want to withdraw.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/slp_wi_2.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

3. Approve the connection request for the liquidity hub canister.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/slp_wi_3.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

4. Once the request is approved, the staking will begin.

<Callout>NOTE: It may take up to 30 seconds complete the withdraw. You will receive a notification when the withdrawing is successful.</Callout>

5. Once the stakeing is successful, you can view the Staked Liquidity History.
