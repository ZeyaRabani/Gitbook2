After receiving the BIT10.BTC token, navigate to [testnet.bit10.app/swap](https://testnet.bit10.app/swap). Here you can swap your BIT10.BTC token to receive Test BIT10.DEFI token(s).

### How to swap

1. Select the number of Test BIT10 token you want and specify the amount of BIT10 tokens.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_1.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

2. Click on the Swap button to buy Test BIT10 token using your BIT10.BTC.

3. Approve the connection request for the token and swap canister.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_2.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

4. Then approve the ICRC2 Approve request to approve the token transfer.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_3.png' alt='' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

5. Once the request is approved, the swap will begin.

<Callout>NOTE: It may take up to 30 seconds to complete the swap. You will receive a notification when the swap is successful.</Callout>

6. Once the swap is successful, navigate to the **Portfolio** page to see the result of your transaction under the **Your Recent Activity** section.
