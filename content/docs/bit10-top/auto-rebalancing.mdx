The S&P 500 works because it adapts:

- Companies that grow get added
- Companies that shrink get removed
- Winners rise in weight
- Losers fade

BIT10 TOP does the same thing. Every day, we:

1. Snapshot current market caps
2. Calculate new weights
3. Adjust holdings to match
4. Your portfolio stays aligned automatically

## Daily Rebalancing at 11AM GMT

BIT10 TOP rebalances daily. This means your portfolio is always reflecting the latest market cap data. Unlike monthly rebalancing (Bitwise) which can leave your portfolio misaligned for weeks, or governance-driven rebalancing (Reserve) which depends on proposal cycles, BIT10's daily approach ensures maximum responsiveness.

Daily rebalancing is the gold standard for volatile markets like crypto, where market cap rankings can shift dramatically in a matter of days. BIT10's smart contracts handle this automatically, so you never need to worry about your portfolio drifting from optimal weights.

## Real Example

NVIDIA wasn't in the S&P 500 in 2017. Today it's the largest company. Kodak was a giant in the 1990s. It was removed in 2004. The index adapts. BIT10 TOP adapts too.
