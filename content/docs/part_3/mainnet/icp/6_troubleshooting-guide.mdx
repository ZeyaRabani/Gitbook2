title: Troubleshooting Guide
---

#### Token Not Visible in Wallet

If the token is not visible in your wallet, follow the steps on the [**Adding Tokens to Display in Your Wallet**](2_adding-tokens-to-display-in-your-wallet) page.

#### Error During Swap After Connection Request

If you encounter an error while swapping BIT10, check the balance of your 'Paying with' token (the token chosen for the swap). If you do not have sufficient 'Paying with' tokens, the swap will fail. Check the **Your recent activity** section on your [Portfolio](https://bit10.app/portfolio) page to verify if the swap was successful.

#### Test BIT10 Tokens Visible on Portfolio Page but Not in Wallet

If your Test BIT10 tokens are visible on the Portfolio page but not in your wallet, please wait 5-10 minutes for the wallet to update. Sometimes, wallets take time to reflect the updated balance.

#### How to Check Transaction Status

For information about your transactions, click the **View Transaction** button under the **Your Recent Activity** section on the Portfolio page to check the transaction details on the BIT10 Explorer.

#### Showing "Infinite" in the "Pay with" Section and Swap Button Disabled

If the app is not fetching the latest price of Test BIT10 tokens or the selected "Pay with" token, it may display an "Infinity" error and disable the Swap button.
Troubleshooting Steps:
- Ensure you are connected to a stable internet network.
- Try using a VPN if the issue persists.
