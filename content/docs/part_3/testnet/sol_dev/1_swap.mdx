After receiving the SOL token on Devnet from Solana Devnet faucet like [faucet.solana.com](https://faucet.solana.com), navigate to [testnet.bit10.app/swap](https://testnet.bit10.app/swap). Here you can swap your SOL token to receive Test BIT10 token(s).

### How to swap

1. Select the number of Test BIT10 token you want and specify the amount of BIT10 tokens.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_sol_dev_1.png' alt='Swap interface' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

2. Click on the Swap button to buy Test BIT10 token.

3. Confirm the transaction to create a new token account for you address.

<Callout>NOTE: If your address already has a token account, the program will automatically move you to the next step.</Callout>

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_sol_dev_2.png' alt='Creating token account' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

4. Then confirm the transaction for swapping token.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_sol_dev_3.png' alt='Transaction approve' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

5. Once the request is approved, the swap will begin.

<Callout>NOTE: It may take up to 30 seconds to complete the swap. You will receive a notification when the swap is successful.</Callout>

6. Once the swap is successful, navigate to the **Portfolio** page to see the result of your transaction under the **Your Recent Activity** section.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_sol_dev_4.png' alt='Your Recent Activity' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>
