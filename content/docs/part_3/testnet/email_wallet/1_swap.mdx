Follow the steps below to use the Testnet with your Email and perform a token swap.

### How to connect

1. Click the **Connect Wallet** button at the top left.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_email_wallet_1.png' alt='Connect Wallet' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

2. Select the **Connect with Email** option to use the Testnet.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_email_wallet_2.png' alt='Connect with Email' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

3. Enter your email and then input the code sent to your inbox to confirm.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_email_wallet_3.png' alt='Enter email and code' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

### How to swap

1. Navigate to the [Email Wallet](https://testnet.bit10.app/newbie) tab and copy your generated Solana Devnet address. Go to a Solana Devnet faucet like [faucet.solana.com](https://faucet.solana.com), and request some Devnet SOL, then navigate to [testnet.bit10.app/swap](https://testnet.bit10.app/swap). Here you can swap your SOL token to receive Test BIT10 token(s).

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_email_wallet_4.png' alt='Swap interface' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

2. Select the number of Test BIT10 token you want and specify the amount of BIT10 tokens.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_email_wallet_5.png' alt='Select tokens' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>

3. Click on the Swap button to buy Test BIT10 token.

4. The transaction will start automatically. Once approved, your swap will be processed.

<Callout>NOTE: It may take up to 30 seconds to complete the swap. You will receive a notification when the swap is successful.</Callout>

5. Once the swap is successful, navigate to the **Portfolio** page to see the result of your transaction under the **Your Recent Activity** section.

<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/testnet_swap_sol_dev_4.png' alt='Your Recent Activity' className='w-full rounded' align='center' width={500} height={500} />
</p>
