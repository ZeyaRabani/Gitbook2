BIT10 is an asset manager that lets users invest in multiple cryptocurrencies at once through **index funds**.
Instead of picking individual coins, you buy one token that represents a group of top-performing assets.

The problem for our users is the amount of research that needs to be done to find the right investment and the high fees buying the cryptocurrencies. This can also mean that the portfolio is unbalanced where he may have all his eggs in one basket increasing risk. And holding some tokens for a long time will have some that are dragging down his portfolio.

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/problem.png' alt='Problem' className='w-full rounded' width={500} height={500} />

The solution that BIT10 offers is it will have a pre-picked basket of assets that the user can hold simply by buying one token. This would reduce the costs and time spent researching, you'll invest in a range of different assets including Bitcoin DeFi, memecoins, top cryptocurrencies, and BRC20 tokens to diversify, and an auto-rebalancing mechanism to replace bad performing tokens with good ones.
