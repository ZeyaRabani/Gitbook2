BIT10 is the "S&P500 of Crypto." BIT10 offers investors a simplified yet powerful solution for accessing and diversifying their exposure to the world of Bitcoin DeFi, memecoins, top cryptocurrencies, and everything else in between.

### **The Problem**

The problem for the user is the amount of research that needs to be done to find the right investment and the high fees buying the cryptocurrencies. This can also mean that the portfolio is unbalanced where he may have all his eggs in one basket increasing risk. And holding some tokens for a long time will have some that are dragging down his portfolio.

<ImageZoom src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/problem.png" className="w-full rounded" width={500} height={500} />

### **The Solution**

The solution that BIT10 offers is it will have a pre-picked basket of assets that the user can hold simply buying one token. This would reduce the costs and time spent researching, you'll invest in a range of different assets including Bitcoin DeFi, memecoins, top cryptocurrencies, and BRC20 tokens to diversify, and an auto-rebalancing mechanism to replace bad performing tokens with good ones.
