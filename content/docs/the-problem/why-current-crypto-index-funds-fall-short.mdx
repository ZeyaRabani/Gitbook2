BIT10's competitors include Index Coop and Reserve Protocol, which offer on-chain index products but are limited to specific chains, lacking true cross-chain functionality. Centralized options like Bitwise provide off-chain ETFs with custody issues. In contrast, BIT10 builds on these by delivering decentralized, cross-chain solutions without bridges or wrappers, with native assets and over-collateralization.

| Competitor | Key Features | Limitations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Index Coop | On-chain indexes on Ethereum | Chain-specific, high gas fees, wrapped assets |
| Reserve Protocol | Decentralized token folios and indexes (DTFs) | Ethereum-only ecosystem, governance complexity, wrapped asset exposure |
| Bitwise | Crypto ETFs | Off-chain custody, not native to crypto principles, accredited investors only |

## Index Coop

Index Coop offers on-chain index funds but is limited to:

- Ethereum mainnet only
- High gas fees during congestion
- Wrapped assets instead of native holdings

## Reserve Protocol

While decentralized, Reserve Protocol faces significant challenges:

- **Ethereum-only ecosystem** - Reserve's RTokens and DTFs operate primarily on Ethereum and Base, lacking true cross-chain support for assets like native BTC or SOL
- **Governance complexity** - Each RToken requires active governance from RSR stakers, creating complex decision-making processes that can slow down rebalancing and parameter adjustments
- **Wrapped asset exposure** - Reserve's basket collateral relies heavily on wrapped and synthetic versions of assets (e.g., wstETH, cbETH), introducing counterparty risk rather than holding native assets directly

## Bitwise (BITW)

The most well-known centralized option:

- Requires accredited investor status
- Off-chain custody with custodian
- 2.5% annual management fee
- No on-chain verification of reserves

## What BIT10 Does Differently

BIT10 builds on the lessons from all these approaches by delivering:

- Decentralized, cross-chain solutions without bridges or wrappers
- Native assets (actual BTC, ETH, SOL)
- 110% over-collateralization
- On-chain verification 24/7
