We plan to build several index tokens to represent different categories of asset on the Bitcoin chain.

1. **BIT10.DEFI**: This token focuses on the top 6 decentralized finance (DeFi) projects on the Bitcoin network. By aggregating these assets, BIT10.DEFI offers investors a streamlined way to participate in the growing DeFi ecosystem, which includes lending, borrowing, and yield farming opportunities.
2. **BIT10.BRC20**: This token will track the top 3 BRC20 tokens, which are built on Bitcoin's Layer 1. BIT10.BRC20 aims to highlight the most innovative projects in the BRC20 space, providing investors with a focused approach to capitalize on the potential of these emerging assets.
3. **BIT10.TOP**: This token will track the top 10 cryptocurrencies, excluding stable coins. It aims to provide investors with exposure to the most significant and promising digital assets in the market, allowing for a diversified investment strategy without the influence of stable coins.
4. **BIT10.MEME**: This token will track the top 10 memecoins, which have gained popularity and community support. BIT10.MEME allows investors to engage with the lighter side of cryptocurrency, capturing the essence of internet culture while providing exposure to potentially high-reward assets.
5. **BIT10.CUSTOM**: This token allows users to create their own index funds by selecting a combination of cryptocurrencies that align with their investment strategies and risk tolerance. These index funds will be voted by the BIT10 community on whether to take them forward by providing liquidity.
