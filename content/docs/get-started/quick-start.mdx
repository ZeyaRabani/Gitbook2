Getting started with BIT10 TOP takes less than 5 minutes.

## Connect Wallet

Here is a guide to connecting your wallet on different chains.

### ICP (Internet Computer)

1. Click the **Connect Wallet** button in the top-right corner.

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/connect-wallet.png' alt='Connect Wallet' className='w-full rounded' width={1920} height={1197} />

2. Choose **Internet Computer**, then select **Plug wallet**.

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/choose-chain.png' alt='Choose Chain' className='w-full rounded' width={1920} height={1197} />

We are using the Plug wallet as our primary wallet for ICP. You can install the Plug wallet extension in your browser by visiting [plugwallet.ooo](https://plugwallet.ooo/).

#### Setting Up Plug Wallet

After installing the Plug wallet extension, open it. Here are some key terms:

1. **Principal ID**: Your unique wallet identifier generated for each wallet.
2. **Account ID**: Derived from the Principal ID, used in the ICP and NFT registries to identify owners.
3. **Manage**: Click this button to select the tokens you want to display in your wallet.

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/plug-key-terms.png' alt='Plug' className='w-full rounded' width={802} height={1404} />

#### Adding BIT10 Tokens to Your Wallet

If your BIT10 tokens are not visible in your wallet after receiving them:

1. Open your Plug wallet and click on the **Manage** button.

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/plug-wallet-manage.png' alt='Plug Manage' className='w-full rounded' width={802} height={1404} />

2. Click on the **Add Token** icon at the top right of the wallet.

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/plug-add-new-token.png' alt='Plug Add' className='w-full rounded' width={802} height={1420} />

3. Enter the following details:
   - **BIT10 TOP** 
      - Canister ID: `g37b3-lqaaa-aaaap-qp4hq-cai` 
      - Standard: `ICRC-1`

4. Enable the button to show the custom token.

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/plug-enable-token.png' alt='Plug Add' className='w-full rounded' width={443} height={745} />

## How to Buy BIT10

To buy BIT10 tokens, navigate to [bit10.app/swap](https://bit10.app/swap).

1. Select the number and type of BIT10 tokens you want to receive, along with the token you want to spend for the swap.

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/select-mint-tokens.png' alt='Select the number and type of BIT10' className='w-full rounded' width={1920} height={1197} />

2. Review the summary and click the **Swap** button to execute the swap. Some tokens may require you to approve the transaction before swapping.

<Callout> **NOTE:** It may take 2–3 minutes to complete the swap. You will receive a notification when the swap is successful. </Callout>

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/summary-and-mint.png' alt='Summary and Mint' className='w-full rounded' width={1920} height={1197} />

3. Once the swap is successful, navigate to the **Portfolio** page to see the result of your transaction under the **Your Recent Activity** section.

## How to Sell BIT10

1. Navigate to the BIT10 Swap page [bit10.app/swap?mode=sell](https://www.bit10.app/swap?mode=sell) and click the **Sell** button.

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/sell-button.png' alt='Sell button' className='w-full rounded' width={1920} height={1197} />

2. Select the number and type of BIT10 tokens you want to send, along with the token you want to receive.

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/select-sell-tokens.png' alt='Select the number and type of BIT10' className='w-full rounded' width={1920} height={1197} />

3. Review the summary and click the **Sell** button. Some tokens may require you to approve the transaction before selling.

<Callout> **NOTE:** It may take 2–3 minutes to complete the sale. You will receive a notification when the sale is successful. </Callout>

<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/summary-and-sell.png' alt='Summary and Sell' className='w-full rounded' width={1920} height={1197} />

4. Once the sale is successful, navigate to the Portfolio page to see the result.

## Troubleshooting Guide

### Fix Failed Mint or Sell
If your inbound transaction succeeded but you didn't receive the swapped token, click the **"Swap Didn't Go Through?"** button at the bottom of the DEX page. Enter the required details and the transaction hash of the first transaction. Once verified, the swap will be processed.

### Error During Mint or Sell After Connection Request
If you encounter an error while swapping tokens, check your "Pay with" token balance. If you do not have sufficient balance, the swap will fail.

### BIT10 Tokens Visible on Portfolio Page but Not in Wallet
Please wait 5–10 minutes for the wallet to update. Sometimes, wallets take time to reflect the updated balance.

### Showing "Infinity" in the "Pay with" Section
If the app is not fetching the latest price of BIT10 tokens or the selected "Pay with" token, it may display an "Infinity" error and disable the Swap button. Troubleshooting steps:
- Ensure you are connected to a stable internet network.
- Try using a VPN if the issue persists.
