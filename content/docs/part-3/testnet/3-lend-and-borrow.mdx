After receiving testnet or BIT10 tokens, navigate to [testnet.bit10.app/lend-and-borrow](https://testnet.bit10.app/lend-and-borrow). Here, you can lend tokens and borrow assets on another chain by using BIT10 tokens as collateral.

<iframe width="560" height="415" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/DCkX21094Ng" title="BIT10 Lend and Borrow" frameBorder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen loading="lazy" className='w-full rounded-md' />

## How to Lend

1. Select the token you want to lend by clicking the <b>Lend Token</b> button.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/lend_and_borrow/lend_token.png' alt='Lend token' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
2. Enter the amount you want to lend.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/lend_and_borrow/enter_lending_amount.png' alt='Enter lending amount' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
3. Review the lending details and click <b>Lend</b>. Some tokens may require you to approve the transaction before lending; approve the transaction, then proceed.

<Callout>NOTE: The minimum lock-in period for lending is 7 days. Once 7 days have passed, you can withdraw the full amount or continue claiming interest.</Callout>
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/lend_and_borrow/lend.png' alt='Lend' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
4. After your lending transaction is successful, navigate to the Portfolio page to see the results under the <b>Your recent lending activity</b> section on the <b>Lending and Borrowing</b> tab. From there, you can manage your position: <b>Withdraw</b> or <b>Claim Interest</b>.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/lend_and_borrow/lending_portfolio.png' alt='Lending portfolio' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={992} />
</p>

## How to Borrow

1. Select the token you want to borrow by clicking the <b>Borrow Token</b> button.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/lend_and_borrow/borrow_token.png' alt='Borrow token' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
2. Select the BIT10 token to use as collateral, then enter the collateral amount and the amount to borrow.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/lend_and_borrow/enter_borrowing_details.png' alt='Enter details' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
3. Review the borrowing details and click <b>Borrow</b>. Some tokens may require you to approve the transaction before borrowing; approve the transaction, then proceed.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/lend_and_borrow/borrow.png' alt='Borrow' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
4. After your borrowing transaction is successful, navigate to the Portfolio page to see the results under the <b>Your recent borrowing activity</b> section on the <b>Lending and Borrowing</b> tab. From there, you can manage your position: <b>Repay</b> or <b>Add Collateral</b>.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/lend_and_borrow/borrow_portfolio.png' alt='Borrow' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
