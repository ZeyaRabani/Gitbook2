After receiving testnet tokens, navigate to [testnet.bit10.app/buy](https://testnet.bit10.app/buy). Here, you can buy different BIT10 tokens.

## How to Buy BIT10

1. Select the number and type of BIT10 tokens you want to receive, along with the token you want to spend for the swap.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/buy/buy_bit10.png' alt='Buy BIT10' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
2. Click the settings icon in the top-right corner to adjust the slippage according to your needs, or keep the default.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/buy/set_slippage.png' alt='Set slippage' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
3. Review the summary and click the <b>Swap</b> button to execute the swap. Some tokens may require you to approve the transaction before swapping; approve the transaction, then execute the swap.

<Callout>NOTE: It may take 2–3 minutes to complete the swap. You will receive a notification when the swap is successful.</Callout>
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/buy/summary_and_swap.png' alt='Summary and Swap' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={1075} />
</p>
4. Once the swap is successful, navigate to the Portfolio page to see the results of your transaction under the <b>Your recent activity</b> section on the <b>BIT10 Portfolio</b> tab.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/buy/bit10_portfolio.png' alt='BIT10 Portfolio' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={2257} />
</p>
