After receiving testnet tokens, navigate to [testnet.bit10.app/swap](https://testnet.bit10.app/swap). Here, you can use our Cross-Chain Swap to swap tokens across multiple chains.

<iframe width="560" height="400" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/sEe__ZwVW8U" title="BIT10 DEX demo" frameBorder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen loading="lazy" className='w-full rounded-md border dark:border-white border-1.5' />

## How to swap

1. Select the tokens or chains, and the amount you want to swap.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/dex/select_token.png' alt='Select token' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={1082} />
</p>
2. Click the settings icon in the top-right corner to adjust the slippage according to your needs, or keep the default.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/dex/set_slippage.png' alt='Set slippage' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={1082} />
</p>
3. Enter the recipient address if performing a cross-chain swap.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/dex/recipient_address.png' alt='Enter recipient address' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={1082} />
</p>
4. Review the summary and click the <b>Swap</b> button to execute the swap. Some tokens may require you to approve the transaction before swapping; approve the transaction, then execute the swap.

<Callout>NOTE: It may take 2–3 minutes to complete the swap. You will receive a notification when the swap is successful.</Callout>
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/dex/summary_and_swap.png' alt='Summary and Swap' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={1300} />
</p>
5. Once the swap is successful, navigate to the Portfolio page to see the results of your transaction under the <b>Your recent swap activity</b> section on the <b>DEX Activity</b> tab.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/testnet/dex/dex_portfolio.png' alt='DEX Portfolio' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1919} height={910} />
</p>
