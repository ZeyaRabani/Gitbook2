To buy or sell BIT10 tokens, navigate to [bit10.app/swap](https://bit10.app/swap). Here, you can buy different BIT10 tokens.

## How to Buy BIT10

1. Select the number and type of BIT10 tokens you want to receive, along with the token you want to spend for the swap.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/mainnet/buy/buy_select_token.png' alt='Select token' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
2. Review the summary and click the <b>Swap</b> button to execute the swap. Some tokens may require you to approve the transaction before swapping; approve the transaction, then execute the swap.

<Callout>NOTE: It may take 2–3 minutes to complete the swap. You will receive a notification when the swap is successful.</Callout>
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/mainnet/buy/buy_summary_and_swap.png' alt='Summary and Swap' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={997} />
</p>
3. Once the swap is successful, navigate to the Portfolio page to see the result of your transaction under the Your Recent Activity section.

## How to Sell BIT10

1. Navigate to the BIT10 Swap page [bit10.app/swap?mode=sell](https://www.bit10.app/swap?mode=sell) and click the Sell button to sell your BIT10 tokens.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/mainnet/buy/select_sell.png' alt='Select sell' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
2. Select the number and type of BIT10 tokens you want to send, along with the token you want to receive.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/mainnet/buy/sell_select_token.png' alt='Select token' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={912} />
</p>
3. Review the summary and click the <b>Sell</b> button. Some tokens may require you to approve the transaction before selling; approve the transaction, then proceed with the sale.

<Callout>NOTE: It may take 2–3 minutes to complete the sale. You will receive a notification when the sale is successful.</Callout>
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/mainnet/buy/sell_summary_and_swap.png' alt='Summary and Swap' className='rounded border' align='center' width={1920} height={997} />
</p>
4. Once the sale is successful, navigate to the Portfolio page to see the result of your transaction under the Your Recent Activity section.
