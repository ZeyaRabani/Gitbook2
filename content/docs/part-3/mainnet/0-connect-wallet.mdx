Here is a guide to connecting your wallet on different chains. The next few pages will guide you through setting up and using the BIT10 Mainnet.

## ICP

For the BIT10 Mainnet, we will use the Internet Computer blockchain.

1. Click the Connect Wallet button in the top-right corner.

We are using the Plug wallet as our primary wallet for the Mainnet. You can install the Plug wallet extension in your browser by visiting [plugwallet.ooo](https://plugwallet.ooo).

### Setting Up Plug Wallet

After installing the Plug wallet extension, open it. Here are some key terms:
1. <b>Principal ID</b>: This is your unique wallet identifier generated for each wallet.
2. <b>Account ID</b>: This is derived from the Principal ID and is used in the ICP and NFT registries to identify the owners of ICP and NFTs.
3. <b>Manage</b>: Click this button to select the tokens you want to display in your wallet.

### Adding Tokens to Display in Your Wallet

After recieving BIT10 tokens if these token are not visible on your wallet then follow these steps:
1. Open your Plug wallet and click on the **Manage** button.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/mainnet/manage_tokens_plug.png' alt='Manage tokens on wallet' className='rounded border w-3/4 md:w-1/3' align='center' width={802} height={1404} />
</p>
2. Click on the Add Token icon at the top right of the wallet.
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/mainnet/add_new_token_plug.png' alt='Add token on wallet' className='rounded border w-3/4 md:w-1/3' align='center' width={802} height={1420} />
</p>
3. Enter the following details for the tokens:
   1. BIT10.TOP
      - Cainster ID: `g37b3-lqaaa-aaaap-qp4hq-cai`
      - Select Standard: `ICRC-1`
<p align='center'>
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/mainnet/add_token_plug.png' alt='Add token' className='rounded border w-3/4 md:w-1/3' align='center' width={802} height={1344} />
</p>
4. Enable the button to show the custom token.
