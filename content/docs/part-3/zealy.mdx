Complete Quests on Zealy to Earn Rewards. Follow the steps below to join Zealy and start completing quests:
1. Go to [zealy.io/cw/bit10cryptoindexfunds/questboard](https://zealy.io/cw/bit10cryptoindexfunds/questboard) and Connect to Zealy.
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/zealy/zealy_1.png' alt='Connect to Zealy' className='rounded border' width={1920} height={912} />

2. Click "Join BIT10 Crypto Index Fund" to become a member.
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/zealy/zealy_2.png' alt='Join BIT10' className='rounded border' width={1920} height={912} />

3. To complete a quest, select it and follow the instructions (e.g., <i>Connect your Twitter, Join Telegram,</i> etc.).
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/zealy/zealy_3.png' alt='Join Quest' className='rounded border' width={1920} height={912} />

4. After completing the task, click Claim to receive your reward and climb the leaderboard.
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/zealy/zealy_4.png' alt='Claim Reward' className='rounded border' width={1920} height={912} />
<ImageZoom src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZeyaRabani/Gitbook2/main/public/assets/part_3/zealy/zealy_5.png' alt='Climb leaderboars' className='rounded border' width={1920} height={912} />
