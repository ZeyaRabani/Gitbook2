Users select the amount, deposit stablecoins, and the system calculates supply, buys assets via liquidity providers, stores them, and mints tokens-all in one transaction for efficiency.

## The Minting Process

### Step 1: Select Amount
Choose how much you want to invest (minimum ~$10)

### Step 2: Deposit Stablecoins
Send USDC or USDT from your wallet

### Step 3: Automatic Processing
BIT10 smart contracts:
- Calculate current prices via oracle
- Determine market-cap weights
- Acquire all 10 assets via DEXs
- Store assets in secure canisters
- Mint BIT10 TOP tokens

### Step 4: Receive Tokens
BIT10 TOP appears in your wallet within seconds

## What Makes It Efficient

**Traditional approach:**
- 10 separate transactions
- 10 approval confirmations
- Multiple gas fees
- Hours of work

**BIT10 approach:**
- 1 transaction
- 1 confirmation
- 1 fee
- Seconds to complete
