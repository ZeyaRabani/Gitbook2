The **BIT10 SOL Idea Token** is our first step toward validating what we build next for BIT10 - in the open ✍️

With **BIT10 TOP now fully live and shipping**, we’re turning our focus to what comes next.

This Idea Token is how we decide.

---

## What is the Idea Token?

The BIT10 SOL Idea Token is **not the BIT10 SOL index fund**.

It does **not** represent ownership in the index, underlying assets, or any treasury. It is **not backed by collateral** and is **not a financial instrument**.

Instead, it exists for one simple reason:

> **To measure market validation**

This is the signal we need to answer:
**Should we build BIT10 SOL?**

---

## Why launch an Idea Token?

Rather than building in isolation, we’re choosing to build **with signal first**.

We’re launching this on EasyA Kickstart to:

- **Validate demand before committing fully**
- **Let the community decide what gets built**
- **Align early supporters with the direction of BIT10**
- **Get a clear signal before going all-in for Colosseum** 🛠️

This gives us real-time insight into:
- Interest  
- Conviction  
- Community alignment  

---

## What is BIT10 SOL?

If validated, **BIT10 SOL** will be:

A **Top 10 Solana Tokens Index Fund** with:
- Daily auto-rebalancing  
- Market cap weighting  
- Market cap pricing  
- Exclusion of stablecoins and wrapped tokens  

It’s the same proven infrastructure behind **BIT10 TOP** - now focused purely on **Solana’s biggest winners** 📊

---

## Important Distinction

- **BIT10 SOL Idea Token → exists today (for validation)**
- **BIT10 SOL Index Fund → only built if validated**

The Idea Token is the **signal**  
The Index Fund is the **product**

---

## Current State

At launch, the Idea Token has **no intrinsic utility** beyond signaling.

- No backing  
- No yield  
- No governance (yet)  
- No guarantees  

This is intentional.

We believe utility should be **earned, not assumed**.

---

## Future Potential

As BIT10 SOL Idea Token gains more validation and traction we will begin building BIT10 SOL the Index Fund in real time and the biggest holder of the token will get:

- Early access to products  
- Community rewards and incentives  
- Governance participation  
- Preferential access to BIT10 assets  

All additions will be **progressive and transparent**.
