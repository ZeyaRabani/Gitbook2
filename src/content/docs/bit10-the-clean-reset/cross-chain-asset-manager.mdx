## How It Works

BIT10 handles:

- **Acquisitions** - Buying the underlying assets
- **Storage** - Secure custody in smart contracts
- **Rebalancing** - Automatic daily adjustments

All via smart contracts on ICP, ensuring users get verifiable, non-custodial ownership of top assets.

## Supported Chains for Minting

| Chain | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- |
| Solana | Fast transactions, low fees, growing DeFi ecosystem |
| Base | Coinbase L2, USDC native, Ethereum security |
| ICP (Internet Computer Protocol) | Native BIT10 chain, chain-key technology |
| BSC (Binance Smart Chain) | Binance ecosystem, low fees, high liquidity |

## Choose Your Chain

You can mint BIT10 TOP from the chain you already use. We're there.

The underlying assets are held natively across chains, while you interact from your preferred ecosystem.
