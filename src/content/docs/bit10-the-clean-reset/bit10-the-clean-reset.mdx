BIT10 envisions a world where crypto investing is as simple as TradFi, with decentralized index funds that provide full market exposure in one transaction.

## The Vision

As a cross-chain asset manager, BIT10 empowers users to own diversified portfolios natively, without custody risks, bridging the gap between crypto's potential and practical accessibility.

## What Makes BIT10 Different

| Feature | Traditional Index | BIT10 |

| Verification | Quarterly reports | Real-time on-chain |
| Accessibility | Accredited investors only | Anyone with $1 of stablecoins |
| Assets | IOUs/Derivatives | Native crypto |
