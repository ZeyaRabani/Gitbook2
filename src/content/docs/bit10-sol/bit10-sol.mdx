BIT10 SOL is the flagship index fund tracking the top 10 cryptocurrencies by market cap (excluding stablecoins and wrapped tokens), providing diversified exposure in a single token.

## Market Cap Weighted

Like the S&P 500, BIT10 SOL is market cap weighted-where larger assets (e.g., SOL, JUP) have more influence.

Think of it as the S&P 500 for crypto: daily rebalancing, no brokerage needed-just a wallet.

## Rebalancing

BIT10 SOL rebalances daily at 11AM GMT. The smart contracts automatically adjust the portfolio weights to reflect the latest market cap data. If an asset grows in market cap, its weight increases; if it shrinks, BIT10 SOL reduces its allocation. This ensures your portfolio is always aligned with the current market-without you lifting a finger.

## Why Market-Cap Weighting?

We weight by market cap for the same reason the S&P 500 does:

- **Larger assets = larger weight** - Bitcoin dominates crypto. It should dominate your portfolio too.
- **Automatic adjustment** - When an asset grows, its weight increases naturally.
- **Proven performance** - Market-cap weighting has outperformed equal weighting and active management for decades.
- **Reduced turnover** - Less frequent rebalancing = lower costs = better returns for you.
