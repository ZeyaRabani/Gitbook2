We call our system "The Mechanism" - it's how you go from holding stablecoins to owning the top 10 crypto market in seconds.

## Step-by-Step Flow

1. **CONNECT** - Connect your wallet (MetaMask, Phantom, Trust Wallet, etc.)
2. **DEPOSIT** - Deposit stablecoins (USDC or USDT)
3. **ACQUIRE** - BIT10 smart contracts automatically acquire the top 10 assets
4. **RECEIVE** - You receive BIT10 TOP tokens in your wallet
5. **HOLD** - Your portfolio auto-rebalances daily

## What Happens Behind the Scenes

When you deposit stablecoins:

- **Price calculation** - Our oracle fetches current prices for all top 10 assets
- **Weight calculation** - Market caps determine allocation percentages
- **Asset acquisition** - Smart contracts acquire native assets via integrated DEXs
- **Collateral storage** - Assets are stored in secure canisters/contracts
- **Token minting** - BIT10 TOP tokens are minted to your wallet
- **Verification** - All transactions recorded on-chain for transparency

All of this happens automatically. You just deposit and receive.

## The Burn Mechanism (Selling)

When you sell or redeem BIT10 TOP tokens, the reverse process occurs:

```text
┌─────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐
│  You Submit  │ ──▶ │  BIT10 Sells │ ──▶ │ You Receive  │
│  BIT10 TOP   │     │ Underlying   │     │  Stablecoins │
└─────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └─────────────┘
```

1. You submit your BIT10 TOP tokens for redemption
2. Smart contracts sell the proportional underlying assets
3. BIT10 TOP tokens are burned (removed from circulation)
4. You receive stablecoins back in your wallet

No lockups. No waiting periods. Your tokens, your choice.

## How Rebalancing Works

BIT10's backend constantly monitors the performance of the cryptocurrencies in the index fund. We track the top 10 cryptos (excluding stablecoins) for BIT10 TOP.

If a crypto falls out of the top 10 due to a drop in market cap, it is removed from the fund and a new cryptocurrency that was previously in position 11 takes its place. Rebalancing occurs daily at 11AM GMT.

BIT10 uses market cap weighting as opposed to a simple average.

### Automatic Rebalancing

Like the S&P 500, BIT10 TOP adapts:

- Assets that grow get added weight
- Assets that shrink lose weight

### Daily Rebalancing Schedule

**Snapshot:** Current market caps recorded, new target weights calculated (e.g., BTC 69.91%, ETH 14.35%, etc.)

**Adjustment:** Compare current holdings to targets, calculate required trades, execute buys/sells to align weights

**Hold:** Portfolio matches market-cap weights. Repeat every day at 11AM GMT.

### Real Example

NVIDIA wasn't in the S&P 500 in 2017. Today it's the largest company. Kodak was a giant in the 1990s. It was removed in 2004. The index adapts. BIT10 TOP adapts too.
