BIT10 was built by two builders who got tired of the same problem every crypto investor faces: too many tokens, too much noise, too little time. They didn't come from hedge funds or VC firms: they came from the code editor, the hackathon floor, and years spent in the trenches of DeFi. That's what makes them the right people to build onchain crypto index funds.

---

## Zeya Rabani - Co-Founder & CEO

### Background

Zeya has been embedded in blockchain since 2021, starting as a smart contract developer focused on DeFi protocols and Solidity before founding BIT10. Prior to BIT10, he worked as a Blockchain Developer at Krossways in London, where he built DeFi applications, deployed tokens on Binance Smart Chain, and integrated cross-chain asset transfers. He also spent years as a full-stack freelance developer building end-to-end web and AI-powered solutions before deciding to go all-in on building the decentralized index fund he wanted to use himself.

### What He Builds at BIT10

Zeya leads product, strategy, and technical architecture. Together with CTO he launched BIT10 TOP on Mainnet in Q2 2025 and developed the auto-rebalancing mechanism, market cap weighting model, and cross-chain pricing infrastructure. He also runs structured user interviews with real investors to drive product-market fit making BIT10 one of the most user-research-backed DeFi startups in its category.

### Why He Started BIT10

> *"We hated crypto picking. We love diversified index funds. The existing cross-chain solutions didn't exist: so we built it."* - Zeya Rabani

## Harshal Raikwar - Co-Founder & CTO

### Background

Harshal is a full-stack and Web3 engineer with deep experience across the entire stack: from smart contracts in Rust on ICP to React frontends and DevOps pipelines. He graduated from SRM Institute of Science and Technology with a B.Tech in Computer Science and has held engineering roles spanning blockchain, AI/ML, and cloud infrastructure. Notably, he was an MLH Fellow at Solana Labs (Summer 2022), where he built core features for the Solana FM block explorer.

### What He Builds at BIT10

Harshal is the engineering backbone of BIT10. He built and maintains the platform's ICP canisters, oracle infrastructure, ICRC token standards, and the full-stack web application using Next.js, TypeScript, Rust, and tRPC. His work on BIT10's cross-chain architecture is what makes native-asset index funds technically possible without wrapped tokens.

### Why He's the Right Engineer for This

Harshal spent his entire engineering career building on the bleeding edge of Web3, from Solana and Ethereum to ICP, giving him a rare ability to reason across multiple chains simultaneously. His background in DevOps and CI/CD ensures that BIT10's infrastructure is not just functional, but production-grade.
